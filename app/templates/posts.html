Stevenage v Reading

Here is my review of Reading FC in August

Now moving onto our stats, first we will look at our possession per game. Looking at this, we can see there is no clear pattern, with games varying from 60% to 40%, averaging 51%. While we do not maintain a consistent possession, we can see how patterns emerge when looking at our opponents and the game stats. For example, in the Bromley game, we have a high percentage due to the fact Bromley are more defensive, and also went a man down with 11 minutes left. The same can be said about wimbldeon, where they set up deeper and more defensive, allowing our players to have more time on the ball in our own half. This can be seen in the Stevenage game, where after we scored, we went more defensive to keep our lead. This worked well, as it meant we were more secure, and allowed us to counter more effectively. The same cant be said about the luton game, where after scoring we went more defensive, we were unable to hold off their superior attack, and were also unable to get back into the game This does show off the versatility of our counter attacking football, being able to play it with both more and less possession

Moving on, we now compare our Goals scored vs our Expected Goals. We can see that our avg goals and our avg xG is the practically the same, with both being 1.6 per game. This is a very good thing to have our goals and xg be the same, as it means we are scoring at the same rate we are producing chances. It is better then producing less goals then xG, as that would mean we are likely missing our chances and failing to convert. Producing more Goals then xG might be good as it might suggest we are very clinical, but it could also mean we are getting lucky with our shots, and might not be sustainable. So having the same Goals to xG is a good indicator we are attacking well. We of course want this to be as high as possible, but 1.6 is still very good.

Now moving onto our Conceded goals to vs our expected faced, we can see that our conceded goals are slightly higher then what were expected to concede. This is not a good thing, as it means we are conceding more then we should be , suggesting that we have not been good defending recently. What we should be aiming for is to be lower then our expected faced, as it means we are stopping the attacks going through. The only positive note is that we are avering more goals for then against, same with xG, suggesting we have be the better side on avg across our games.

When looking at our passing averages, its seems we often hover around the same percentages per game. When we compare these stats to our last seasons avg, we avg 75.3% pass completion last season, which means we have improved by 1.5%. Looking at our long balls, we are also up, improving on 32.4%, improving by 2%. When we look at crossing, we are down by a decent amount, were we avg 23.9%, meaning we have decreased by 3.3%. This suggests that we have improved our short and long balls, but we still need to work on getting accurate crosses. I think this is mainly down to us lacking tall options up top to connect with these crosses.

If I had to give this month a rating, I would give it a 5/10. I can see the ideas coming together with this team, but it is definitely not the finished product.
