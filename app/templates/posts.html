Kyrecce Lisbie Welcome Post

Here is a look at some of Kyreece Lisbie's last season stats compared to one of his teammates, Harry Anderson, and a player similar in position and league finish, Calum Agius of Crewe Alexandra

First we look at Goals and Assists where Lisbie is close to his Harry, and is ahead of Calum by a decent amount

Next we look at the shots of each player, in which we can see Lisbie is ahead of both players in all values, showing how he could be consistent in getting shots off and getting them on target

When looking at the players passing, we can see that Lisbie is behind on all passing values, suggesting that he might be more of an attacking winger who focuses on getting down the pitch rather than building up play

Udoka Godwin-Malife

30/06/2026

Here is a look at new signing Udoka Godwin-Malife, compared with some of his new teammates, Paudie O'Connor and Jeriel Dorsett.

Looking at Udoka passing, he is close compared to his standard pass completion to Paudie and Jeriel, while his long balls are much more accurate. His crossing is down compared to the others, maybe suggesting he struggles with crossing, but he has completed more crosses then both.

When comparing the players duel percentages, we can see that his ground duels is below the others, while being between the others when it comes to aerial duels. This could suggest that he is not as confident when it comes to contesting the ball in close quarter situations.

Looking at their defensive contributions, we can see where Udoka excels, with his recoveries miles above the others, highlighting his ability to catch up to long balls and recover the ball. He does fall behind tackles and interceptions compared to the others.

Overall, despite some of the data suggesting he isn't as defensively sound as his teammates, I think he will do well playing alongside Paudie, in a back 2 or 3, with Paudie being able to win more duels and tackles, while Udoka can help with recoveries behind the defensive line.

Reading FC v Bromley

11/08/2026

Here is my review of #readingfc v Bromley

When looking at our attacking stats, we can see we just edge them in our shots, while also getting into the box more then Bromley. Its also worth noting that we had a better xG, 2.39 to Bromley's 1.67.

We can see defensively we were much poorer, only beating them on blocks and clearances. This is poor, as it only shows we weren't stopping the attacks and were unable to keep possession, we want to have higher tackles and interceptions.

When looking at our passing stats, we can see that we did have a higher passing accuracy in both short and long balls, while being slightly less in crossing. The short pass rate isn't great, as it should be in the 80's, but the long and crossing is in the right area.

Overall, the stats do say reading fc were the slightly better team, with the only problem being our defence being caught out a couple of times, which is a problem with sitting deep, as it means the attackers have less distance to goal if they get the ball. Attacking we were good.

Reading FC v Luton

11/08/2026

Here is my review of #readingfc v Luton

Comparing our attacking stats, we were pretty much identical to Luton, only getting 5 more shots then Luton. We also just edged them out on xG, 1.77 to 1.60. Its good that we were matching a high quality opposition, and shows how our attack was decent.

Defensively we were off it, which could be seen on the pitch and in the stats. Interceptions were up on Bromley which was good, but only 1 block and 11 tackles showed how we lacked stopping attacks or shots

Looking at the passing, its disappointing to see our passing and crossing percentage drop from Bromley, and being worse off then Luton. We did get an impressive long ball percentage, which is good for our tactics of getting the ball up quickly, and can be seen in two of our goals.

Overall, We were decent going forward, but lacked in our defensive contributions and passing, going backwards from the Bromley game.
